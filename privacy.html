Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information this Discord bot ("the Bot") may collect, use, and store.

1. Information We Collect

The Bot may process information that is provided to it through Discord commands.

For example, the Bot's /ggtiers-ign command allows a user to enter a Minecraft username (IGN). The submitted IGN may be displayed in the Discord server as part of the Bot's response.

The Bot may also process basic Discord information required for its commands to function, such as:

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord server ID
  • Discord channel ID
  • Command and interaction information

The exact information processed may change as additional features are added to the Bot.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the Bot is used to provide its features and respond to commands.

For example, a Minecraft IGN submitted through /ggtiers-ign is used to create the corresponding Bot message in the Discord server.

The Bot does not intentionally use collected information for advertising or sell personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

The Bot may store information required for its features. If information is stored, it will be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the Bot's functionality.

Information that is no longer necessary may be deleted.

4. Third Parties

The Bot operates through Discord. Information sent through Discord is also subject to Discord's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

The Bot may use third-party hosting or services in the future. If this changes how information is collected, processed, or stored, this Privacy Policy may be updated.

5. Data Security

Reasonable measures will be taken to protect information handled by the Bot. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Bot's features change. The latest version will be made available through the Bot's designated Privacy Policy page.

8. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact the Bot's owner or administrator through the Discord server where the Bot is installed.
